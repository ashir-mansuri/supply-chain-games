Supply Chain Games

Two classroom games about the decisions that keep goods moving. Students play in teams, each on their own phone or laptop, in class or online. Nothing to install.

Dock 1

Supplier Showdown

Buy four parts for an electric scooter maker in Sanand from suppliers in China, Vietnam, India and Germany. Fires, floods, tariffs and chip shortages hit over eight months. Cheap or safe: which one wins?

Players
Teams of 3 to 5, each member on their own device
Time
About 30 minutes of play, plus debrief
Topics
Supplier selection, total cost of ownership, dual sourcing, buffer stock, supply risk
Play Supplier Showdown
Dock 2

Warehouse Rush

Pick customer orders on a warehouse map, then use last month’s sales to move fast sellers next to dispatch, then try batch picking. The same six orders, three times, getting faster each time.

Players
Teams of 2 to 4, taking turns as the picker
Time
About 20 minutes of play, plus debrief
Topics
ABC analysis, Pareto principle, slotting, pick routes, batch picking, productivity
Play Warehouse Rush

Teaching with these games

Create a class with team PINs and follow every team live on the teacher dashboard. The guide has setup steps, lesson plans for a 60-minute class, debrief answers and follow-up work.
