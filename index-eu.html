Supply Chain Games

Nine classroom games about the decisions that keep goods moving. Three team games, played by a team gathered round one phone or laptop, and six solo games, four of them about doing business across borders. Nothing to install.

Team games

A team of two to five gathers round one phone or laptop and decides together.

Supplier Showdown

Buy four parts for an e-bike maker in Leipzig from suppliers in China, Vietnam, Poland and Taiwan. Fires, floods, tariffs and chip shortages hit over eight months. Cheap or safe: which one wins?

Players
A team of 3 to 5, round one phone or laptop
Time
About 30 minutes of play, plus debrief
Topics
Supplier selection, total cost of ownership, dual sourcing, buffer stock, supply risk
Play Supplier Showdown

Warehouse Rush

Black Friday week at a fulfilment centre near Leipzig. Pick customer orders on a warehouse map, then use last month’s sales to move fast sellers next to dispatch, then try batch picking. The same six orders, three times, getting faster each time.

Players
A team of 2 to 4, round one phone or laptop
Time
About 20 minutes of play, plus debrief
Topics
ABC analysis, Pareto principle, slotting, pick routes, batch picking, productivity
Play Warehouse Rush

Coffee Chain

Run one stage of a coffee supply chain, from a roaster in Hamburg to a café in Munich. Order week by week, see only your own stage, and find out what one small change in demand does further up the chain. Modelled on the beer game.

Players
One player against the computer, or a team of 4 round one screen
Time
About 25 minutes of play, plus debrief
Topics
Bullwhip effect, lead times, information sharing, stock versus backlog costs
Play Coffee Chain

Solo games: operations

One player per device.

Halftime Bakes

Run a bakery stall outside a football stadium in Munich during a 10-day summer tournament. Each morning, decide how many brownies and sandwiches to make. Too few and fans walk away; too many and the leftovers lose money.

Players
One player, on any phone or laptop
Time
About 15 minutes, plus debrief
Topics
Perishable stock, uncertain demand, cost of too many versus too few, critical ratio
Play Halftime Bakes

Season Planner

Plan six months of production at a garden furniture maker in Bielefeld, with a big peak as spring arrives. Hire, run overtime, subcontract or build stock early, and meet the orders at the lowest cost.

Players
One player, on any phone or laptop
Time
About 20 minutes, plus debrief
Topics
Aggregate planning, level versus chase strategy, capacity, overtime, subcontracting
Play Season Planner

Solo games: global business

One player per device. Trade, shipping and production decisions across borders.

Export Desk

Ship a Dortmund pump maker’s orders to India, China, the UK and Nigeria. Choose sea, sea-air or air for each order, decide whether to lock the exchange rate, and whether to insure the cargo, while canal blockages, port strikes and currency swings hit along the way.

Players
One player, on any phone or laptop
Time
About 15 minutes, plus debrief
Topics
International logistics, transit time versus cost, currency risk and forward contracts, cargo insurance
Play Export Desk

Go Global

Take a natural skincare brand from Freiburg into the UAE, UK, USA, Japan and Kenya over three years. In each market, choose an online store, a distributor, a joint venture or your own subsidiary, and face tariffs, currency crashes and partner disputes.

Players
One player, on any phone or laptop
Time
About 20 minutes, plus debrief
Topics
Market selection, entry modes, step-by-step internationalisation, country risk
Play Go Global

Factory Footprint

A tile maker in Castellón, Spain, sells in Europe, the Gulf, the UK and the USA. Over six years, decide where to build plants: at home, in the UAE, the UK or Mexico. Compare landed costs as a US duty, a UK carbon fee, a freight spike and a gas cut change the picture.

Players
One player, on any phone or laptop
Time
About 20 minutes, plus debrief
Topics
Global facility location, landed cost, tariffs and carbon border fees, capacity planning, concentration risk
Play Factory Footprint

Freight Buyer

Buy container space for a Hamburg homeware importer bringing goods from Shanghai for two years. Each quarter, book space on contract with a large or a small shipping line, or leave it to the spot market, while freight rates calm, spike and crash.

Players
One player, on any phone or laptop
Time
About 15 minutes, plus debrief
Topics
Freight procurement, contract versus spot, carrier reliability, price volatility
Play Freight Buyer

All companies, products, prices and events in these games are made up for teaching.
